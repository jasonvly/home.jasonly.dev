One thing I've come to realise is that whether we view something as useful or not useful is highly dependent on our situation. Go up, go down, go left go right, don't go, wash your hands, do a spin. 

Former me was always caught up in this eternal binary struggle between two choices. Exercise or relax? Pursue physical or intellectual? EQ or IQ? Red powerade or blue powerade? A or B? A house is burning down? Pour water on it. The burning house is covered in oil? Why the fuck would you pour water on it, you fucking idiot? Didn't you know that adding water to an oil fire is a the dumbest thing you can do?

It is very hard to find universal truths.

Advice is hard.

So what's my answer? Play it by ear. 

Find the context. As you go. Due diligence


Find the feeling that speaks to you.

Knowledge is useful.
